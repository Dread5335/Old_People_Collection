How to Play

Move cards to build the four piles in the top right, from Ace up to King, one suit at a time.

On the seven piles below, cards must go down in number and switch between red and black.

To move a card: tap it once, then tap where you want it to go. Tap it twice quickly to send it straight to its foundation pile, if it fits.

Tap the draw pile (top left) for a new card. An empty draw pile can be tapped again to reuse the waste pile.
