How to Play

Tap a tile to reveal it. A number tells you how many mines are hiding in the 8 tiles touching it. An empty (blank) tile means no mines are nearby, and the surrounding tiles open up automatically.

Marking a mine: turn on "Flag Mode" below the board, then tap a tile you believe hides a mine to plant a flag on it (tap again to remove it). Turn Flag Mode back off to keep revealing tiles.

Quick-clear: if you've flagged all the mines around a revealed number, tap that number twice quickly to open every other tile around it at once.

Reveal every safe tile without revealing a mine to win. If you tap a mine by accident, tap Undo to take it back — no need to lose the whole game over a slip of the finger.
