How to Play

Tiles are stacked in layers. Tap one tile, then tap a second tile with the same rank and suit (like two 7♥ tiles) to clear both from the board.

Only tiles that are free can be tapped: a tile with another tile sitting on top of it is covered and can't be used yet, and a tile squeezed between two neighbors on both sides is stuck until one of those neighbors is cleared. Covered or stuck tiles look dimmed.

Clear every tile to win. If no matching pair is currently free, tap Shuffle Tiles to rearrange what's left into a fresh, solvable layout — that's not a cheat, it's expected.
